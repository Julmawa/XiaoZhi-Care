# Privacy

XiaoZhi Care is designed around a **Local First** model for Care-specific personal information.

## Stored locally

Depending on enabled features, Care may store locally:

- family and contact information;
- personal preferences;
- routines;
- reminders;
- pillbox schedules and confirmation history;
- local audio files;
- Care configuration.

## Important distinction

Local Care storage does **not** mean that every XiaoZhi function is offline.

The base XiaoZhi installation may use external services for:

- speech recognition;
- language models;
- text-to-speech;
- server-side MCP functions;
- other configured cloud services.

Users should review the privacy behavior of the XiaoZhi backend they choose.

## Backups

Installer backups are complete Flash images and may contain:

- Wi-Fi credentials;
- device identifiers;
- Care personal data;
- configuration;
- other private information.

Treat backup `.bin` files as sensitive. Do not upload them to GitHub, Issues, Discussions or public file-sharing services.

## Public bug reports

Before posting logs or screenshots:

- remove names and personal data;
- remove network credentials;
- remove tokens;
- remove device identifiers when unnecessary;
- never upload a complete Flash dump.
